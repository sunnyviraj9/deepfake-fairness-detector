🔥 How Grad-CAM Works

Back-propagates the classification score through the last convolutional layer. Channel-weighted activation maps are summed with ReLU and overlaid on the image using a JET heat-map colourmap.

Red = high activation Amber = medium Blue = low
📡 How FFT Spectrum Works

Decomposes the luminance channel with a 2D Fourier transform. After fftshift, log-magnitude reveals spectral artefacts invisible in the spatial domain — generative model grids appear as bright cross patterns.

Blue = DC / low-freq White = high-freq peaks
