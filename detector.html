🔥 How Grad-CAM Works

Grad-CAM back-propagates the classification score through the last convolutional layer. Gradient magnitudes are averaged per channel (global average pooling) to produce channel importance weights, then summed over channels with ReLU to discard negative contributions. The result is upsampled and overlaid on the original image using the JET colormap.

Red = high activation Amber = medium Blue = low
📡 How FFT Spectrum Works

The 2D Fast Fourier Transform decomposes the luminance channel into frequency components. After fftshift (DC moved to centre), log-magnitude highlights spectral artefacts. Generative models (GANs, diffusion) leave characteristic grid patterns or spectral peaks invisible in the spatial domain but clearly visible here.

Blue = low freq Cyan = mid White = high freq peaks
