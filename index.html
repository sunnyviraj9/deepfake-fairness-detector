⚙️ How It Works

From image to fair decision in four steps

1
📸

Upload Face Image

Drag and drop any face image. It's resized to 224×224 and normalised with ImageNet statistics before inference.

2
🧠

Neural Inference

EfficientNet-B0 processes the image through its pretrained backbone. The custom classification head outputs a binary logit.

3
🔬

XAI Explanation

Grad-CAM backpropagates through the last convolutional layer. The 2D FFT spectrum reveals synthesis artefacts in the frequency domain.

4
⚖️

Fairness Audit

Equalized Odds regularisation and demographic-balanced sampling ensure consistent performance across all 10 Monk Skin Tones.
